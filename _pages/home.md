We are the **IDEA (Impact-Driven Evaluation of AI) Lab** in **NYU Computer Science**, led by Dr. [Emily Black](https://emblack.github.io/).

Our research develops methods to evaluate how AI is used in practice and to mitigate harms from real-world AI systems.

### Our research

Our work spans three connected areas:

1. **Algorithmic fairness and AI safety**  
   We study how AI systems behave across groups and settings, and develop methods to evaluate and reduce harms.

2. **AI and the law / AI governance**  
   We examine how technical design interacts with legal standards, regulation, and policy governance.

3. **Learning from and auditing AI deployments on the ground**  
   We study how AI systems are actually used in practice, and build methods to audit real deployments and their impacts.
